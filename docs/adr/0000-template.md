# ADR 0000: Title in the form of a decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the situation, what forces are in play, and why does a decision have to be made now? Two or three paragraphs at most. Link the spec this decision serves.

## Decision

What we are doing, in one sentence, then the detail. Written as a statement, not a proposal.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we have given up. Name the thing that will bite later. A decision with no listed downside has not been thought through.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative in one or two sentences with the reason it lost. Keep them; they are the most useful part of this file six months from now.
